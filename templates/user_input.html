How it works and FAQ · Strategy guide

How the free DAT angle ranking practice works

  1. Choose an angle difference from 1 to 36 degrees. This is how many degrees apart each angle is from the next. Smaller numbers are harder.
  2. Look at four random angles, labeled 1 to 4. Arm lengths and directions are randomized, just like the real test.
  3. Rank them from smallest to largest and press Submit. If your order is wrong, the correct order is shown.
  4. Play again or adjust. Keep the same difficulty for more practice, or lower the angle difference as your accuracy improves.

Not sure where to start? Try 30 degrees, then work down. The strategy guide covers techniques for close angles, and the practice progression article lays out a step-by-step plan for lowering the difficulty.
