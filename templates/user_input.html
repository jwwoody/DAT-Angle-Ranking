How It Works

  1. Pick a difficulty. The number you enter is the gap in degrees between each angle and the next largest. A 30-degree difference is a good place to start.
  2. Rank the angles. You'll see four angles drawn in random orientations with arms of different lengths. Use the dropdowns to put them in order from smallest to largest.
  3. Check your answer. Press Submit to see if you were right. If not, the correct order is shown so you can see what you missed.
  4. Keep going. Play again at the same difficulty, or lower the difference as your accuracy improves.
