Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This policy explains what information DAT Angle Ranking (www.datangleranking.com) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. You can use the site without creating an account or giving us your name or email address.

Information We Collect

Practice Session Data

When you choose an angle difference and play, we store your chosen angle difference and the answer to your current problem in a session cookie. This cookie is required for the practice tool to work, is not used for tracking, and expires when you close your browser.

Generated Practice Images

Each practice problem is drawn as an image and temporarily saved on our server so it can be shown to you. These images contain only randomly generated angles and no information about you. Older images are deleted automatically as new ones are created.

Ad Frequency Data

On the practice page, we use your browser's local storage to remember when an ad was last shown and how many problems you have submitted since then, so we can limit how often ads appear. This data stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

Server Logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic request information such as IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time of the request. We use these logs only to keep the site secure and to fix errors.

Emails You Send Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond to you.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which keeps the site free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read the Google Privacy Policy.

Cookie Policy

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data saved by your browser. This site uses:

  • session (cookie, strictly necessary, set by us): stores your angle difference and the current problem's answer. It is deleted when you close your browser.
  • lastAdShown and submissionCount (local storage, set by us): limit how often ads appear on the practice page. They remain until you clear your browser's site data.
  • Advertising cookies (set by Google and its partners): used to serve, personalize, and measure ads, as described above.

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Blocking the session cookie will prevent the practice tool from working.

How We Use Information

  • To run the angle ranking practice tool
  • To limit how often ads are shown
  • To keep the site secure and fix problems
  • To respond to messages you send us

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use the information described above for any other purpose.

Data Retention

Session cookies expire when you close your browser. Generated images are deleted automatically. Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and troubleshooting. Emails are kept only as long as needed to respond to you.

Your Choices and Data Deletion Requests

Because the site has no accounts, we do not keep a profile about you. You can remove the data stored in your browser at any time by clearing this site's cookies and site data in your browser settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information about you, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, such as deleting emails you sent us or information in our server logs, email contact@datangleranking.com with the subject "Data deletion request." We will respond within 30 days. Data collected by Google for advertising is controlled by Google and can be managed through the links in the Advertising section above.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information described in this policy. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for pre-dental students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email contact@datangleranking.com or visit our contact page.
